# Storyboard

> Version 1.0

---

# Experience Duration

60–90 Seconds

---

# Story Theme

A magical birthday journey under a beautiful night sky.

The user should feel curiosity, excitement, warmth, and happiness from beginning to end.

---

# Scene 1 — The Invitation

Duration:
5 seconds

Purpose:
Capture attention.

Visuals:

- Dark night sky
- Twinkling stars
- Soft moving particles
- Ambient glow

Animation:

- Fade in from black
- Stars slowly appear
- Soft camera zoom

Text:

"A special surprise is waiting..."

Interaction:

Button

Continue →

Transition:

Zoom into the stars.

---

# Scene 2 — Birthday Reveal

Duration:
8 seconds

Purpose:
Reveal the recipient.

Visuals:

Elegant glowing typography.

Animation:

- Happy Birthday fades in
- Sparkle trail
- Name appears with glow

Text:

Happy Birthday

Summi

Transition:

Soft light expands across the screen.

---

# Scene 3 — The Gift

Duration:
10 seconds

Purpose:
Create interaction.

Visuals:

Luxury gift box.

Animation:

- Floating gently
- Ribbon moves slightly
- Soft shadow

Interaction:

Tap Gift

Result:

- Lid opens
- Golden light escapes
- Particle burst
- Confetti begins

Transition:

Golden light fills screen.
